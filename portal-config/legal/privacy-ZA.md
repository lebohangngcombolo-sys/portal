# Privacy Policy — South Africa

**Effective date:** 11 November 2026
**Last updated:** 11 November 2026
**Data controller:** TradeBridge Africa (Pty) Ltd

This Privacy Policy describes how TradeBridge Africa ("TradeBridge", "we") collects, uses, and shares personal information when you use our platform. It explains your rights under the Protection of Personal Information Act, 4 of 2013 (POPIA).

## 1. Information we collect

We collect the following categories of personal information:

- **Identity data** — name, date of birth, nationality, identity document number, passport details.
- **Contact data** — email address, phone number, physical address.
- **Account data** — credentials (hashed), security settings, device and session information.
- **KYC data** — identity documents, business registration documents, beneficial ownership information, and verification records.
- **Transaction data** — records of services you use, invoices, and payment information.
- **Technical data** — IP address, user agent, and operational logs generated when you use the Platform.
- **Communication data** — support tickets, emails, and notifications.

## 2. How we use your information

We use your personal information for the following purposes:

- **Account management** — to create and manage your account, authenticate you, and provide customer support.
- **KYC and compliance** — to verify your identity, screen against sanctions and PEP lists, and comply with FICA and other applicable laws.
- **Service delivery** — to provide the services you have requested.
- **Security** — to detect and prevent fraud, unauthorized access, and other threats.
- **Communication** — to send you service notifications, security alerts, and updates about the Platform.
- **Legal obligations** — to comply with legal, regulatory, and tax obligations.

We do not sell your personal information.

## 3. Legal basis

Under POPIA, we process your personal information on the following bases:

- **Consent** — where you have given specific, informed consent.
- **Contract** — to perform our agreement with you.
- **Legal obligation** — to comply with FICA and other laws.
- **Legitimate interest** — to prevent fraud, secure the Platform, and improve our services, balanced against your rights.

Where we rely on legitimate interest, we conduct a balancing test and give you the right to object.

## 4. Sharing your information

We share your personal information with the following categories of recipients:

- **Service providers** — identity verification providers, SMS and email delivery providers, cloud infrastructure providers, and analytics providers, under written agreements and confidentiality obligations.
- **Regulators and authorities** — as required by FICA and other applicable laws, including the Financial Intelligence Centre, the South African Revenue Service, and law enforcement agencies where legally compelled.
- **Professional advisers** — auditors, lawyers, and insurers under confidentiality obligations.
- **Corporate transactions** — in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, subject to applicable law.

We do not transfer your personal information across borders except to countries that provide an adequate level of protection under POPIA, or under appropriate safeguards (such as binding corporate rules or standard contractual clauses).

## 5. Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as:

- Your account is active; or
- Required by applicable law. For FICA-related records, the retention period is at least five years after the end of the business relationship.
- Needed to resolve disputes or enforce our agreements.

After the retention period, we securely delete or anonymize your personal information.

## 6. Your rights

Under POPIA, you have the right to:

- **Access** your personal information we hold about you.
- **Correct** inaccurate or incomplete personal information.
- **Delete** personal information that we no longer need for the purpose it was collected, subject to legal retention obligations.
- **Object** to processing based on legitimate interest.
- **Withdraw consent** at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of processing based on consent before its withdrawal.
- **Lodge a complaint** with the Information Regulator.

To exercise these rights, contact our Information Officer at privacy@tradebridge.africa. We will respond within 30 days.

## 7. Security

We use industry-standard technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information, including encryption in transit and at rest, access controls, and regular security assessments. No system is completely secure; you are responsible for safeguarding your account credentials.

## 8. Children

The Platform is not directed at children under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## 9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated via email or through the Platform at least 30 days before taking effect.
